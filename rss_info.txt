================================================================================
RSS FEED RESEARCH — news-app
Date: 2026-07-09
================================================================================

PURPOSE

Identify the best / most popular RSS feeds currently available for the app's
six topics (Geopolitics, Ukraine, Middle East, Markets, Tech, Domestic US),
verify every candidate actually works, and record what was configured and why.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
METHODOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Candidate list built from two directions:
   a. Major outlets per topic (wire services, broadsheets, broadcasters,
      specialist publications).
   b. Web research: RSS directories and "best feeds" roundups current for 2026
      (Feedspot category rankings, FeedWind/Mikle directory, elink roundup),
      plus targeted searches for individual outlets (AP, Meduza).

2. Every URL was LIVE-TESTED on 2026-07-09 through the app's own fetch path
   (backend/app/pipeline/ingest_rss.py fetch_feed_text: httpx, 30s timeout,
   redirects followed, User-Agent "news-app/1.0 (personal RSS reader)").
   A feed only counts as working if it returned HTTP 200 AND feedparser
   extracted at least one entry.

3. Metrics captured per feed: entry count in the feed window, age of newest
   entry, and rough posts/day extrapolated from the timestamps in one
   snapshot. Volume numbers are approximate — one-day snapshots of feeds with
   small windows overstate or understate; treat as order-of-magnitude.

Directory listicles were used only to source candidates, never as evidence a
feed works — several feeds they recommend are dead or unofficial (see
FINDINGS).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. THE MAJOR WIRE SERVICES HAVE NO OFFICIAL PUBLIC RSS.
   - Associated Press: apnews.com feeds discontinued; live test returned
     401/empty. "AP RSS feeds" in directories are third-party generators
     (rss.app, RSSHub, GitHub scrapers) — unofficial and fragile.
   - Reuters: public RSS discontinued; live test returned 401.
   - Bloomberg: no public RSS.
   Directory roundups still list all three, which says something about
   directory quality. Wire coverage has to come indirectly (BBC/Guardian/NPR
   syndicate heavily from wires).

2. ZOMBIE FEEDS EXIST — feeds that return 200 and parse cleanly but are
   abandoned. Both WSJ feeds (RSSWorldNews, RSSMarketsMain via feeds.a.dj.com)
   parse fine but their newest entry is ~18 months old. Without checking entry
   dates these would silently ingest nothing forever.

3. USER-AGENT BLOCKING IS COMMON. CNBC (403) and Yahoo Finance (429) reject
   httpx's default "python-httpx/x" UA but accept an honest identified UA
   ("news-app/1.0 (personal RSS reader)"). This was fixed in the app's
   fetch_feed_text on 2026-07-09. No browser masquerading required.

4. SOME SITES HARD-BLOCK ALL NON-BROWSER CLIENTS. Institute for the Study of
   War (understandingwar.org) returns 403 even with a full Chrome UA
   (Cloudflare-level filtering). Unusable without scraping infrastructure.

5. DATA QUALITY QUIRKS FOUND LIVE:
   - Jerusalem Post publishes future-dated timestamps (entries dated ~3h in
     the future). The app parses them, but recency-decay scoring would
     slightly overrate those items.
   - Meduza's English feed (/rss/en) is discontinued; the only working feed
     (rss2/all) is Russian-language (0 of 30 entries English).
   - Kyiv Independent moved its feed: /rss/ and /feed are 404; the working
     URL is /feed/rss.

6. POPULARITY CONSENSUS FROM 2026 DIRECTORIES/ROUNDUPS. The names that appear
   consistently at the top across Feedspot's ranked categories and the
   FeedWind/elink roundups: BBC, NYT, Guardian, Al Jazeera, NPR, WSJ, plus
   AP/Reuters (dead, see #1); for finance: CNBC, MarketWatch, Investing.com,
   Seeking Alpha, Motley Fool, Bloomberg (dead); international: DW, France 24.
   Note popularity rankings reflect brand recognition, not feed health or
   fit — hence the live testing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT'S CONFIGURED AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

19 feeds (marked [C] above), roughly 250-300 items/day total, balanced across
the app's six topics.

Selection criteria:
- Every topic covered by 2+ independent sources.
- Favor quality-per-item over raw volume: each ingested item costs a Voyage
  embedding, each new story a Haiku filter call, each surviving story a
  Sonnet scoring call. The firehoses (Al Jazeera 128/day, UPI 145/day,
  MEE 114/day, The Hill 96/day, France 24 105/day, MarketWatch ~100/day) were
  left out of the default set; add selectively if a topic feels thin.
- Skipped Hacker News by default: entries are bare external links with no
  body text, which gives the filter/scorer almost nothing to work with.
- Overlap is deliberate where perspectives differ (BBC + Guardian world;
  CNBC + FT + Economist markets; Politico + NPR + Axios for DC).

To add a feed: append {"name": ..., "url": ...} to RSS_FEEDS in
backend/app/config.py. First poll snapshots the feed (no backfill); items
flow from the second poll onward.
